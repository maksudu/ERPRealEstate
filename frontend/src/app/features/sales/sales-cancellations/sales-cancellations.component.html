Real Estate Act & REHAB Statutory Allotment Cancellation Rules

Under Section 14 of the Real Estate Development and Management Act 2010 and REHAB statutory bylaws, upon unilateral cancellation by the allottee, the developer is entitled to deduct a maximum of 10% (Ten Percent) of the total amount deposited as administrative forfeiture charge. The net balance is refunded via account payee cheque within the statutory period, and the allocated unit is automatically restored to AVAILABLE inventory status.
