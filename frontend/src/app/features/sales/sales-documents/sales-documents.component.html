Statutory Legal Notice & Compliance Protocol (Bangladesh Property Law)

The system prepares draft legal instruments (Agreement for Sale, Allotment Letters, Possession & Handover, Deeds) and tracks registration documentation. Final legal instruments must be vetted by authorized legal counsel, executed on non-judicial stamp paper of prescribed value, and registered with the competent Sub-Registry Office in accordance with applicable Bangladesh registration laws. A draft or computer-generated document is not, by itself, a legally executed deed or registered title instrument.

Statutory 9-Point Registration Pre-requisite Checklist

Under Bangladesh Transfer of Property Act, 1882 and Registration Act, 1908, deeds cannot be lodged for Sub-Registry execution without complete clearance of all statutory pre-conditions.

1. Purchaser Identity & Tax Verification

Purchaser NID/Passport, E-TIN Certificate & recent tax return acknowledgment copy, verified passport-size photographs.

2. Financial Clearance & Receipts

100% Booking money realization in escrow bank account, down payment (min 15%) cleared via account-payee instrument.

3. Title, Sanction & Stamp Duty

Non-encumbrance title search at Sub-Registry, RAJUK/CDA/KDA approved building sanction, non-judicial stamp duty & sub-registry token.
