ERP Commission Policy

1. Milestone Prerequisite

Commissions do NOT become payable solely on lead submission. Claims require verified booking or collection milestones.

2. Anti-Duplicate Prevention

System automatically checks and prevents duplicate claims on the same unit/booking across all partners.

3. Bangladesh Tax (TDS 10%)

Under Income Tax Act 2023, 10% TDS (Advance Income Tax) is deducted at source prior to net disbursement.

4. Dual Approvals

All claims route sequentially through Sales Verification and Finance Department authorization before payout.
