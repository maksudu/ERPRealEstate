Investment Committee Review & Governance

Key Financial Indicators & Accounting Basis

The ERP defines the calculation basis for each viability benchmark and explicitly segregates projected figures from post-construction audited accounting ledgers:

Total Development Cost: Land + Construction + Approvals + Marketing + Finance + Contingencies (Accrual basis).
Projected Revenue: Contractual gross realizable value at target price/sft & parking slot rates.
Gross Project Profit: Projected Revenue − Total Development Cost.
Break-even Sales: Sales volume (BDT & SqFt) strictly required to fully recover total development outlay.
Cash Flow: Expected customer collection receipts − expected vendor payments over quarterly milestones.
}
* Feasibility figures remain separate from accounting ledgers until board sanction.
