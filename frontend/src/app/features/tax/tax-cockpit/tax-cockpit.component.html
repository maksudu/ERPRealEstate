Bangladesh Real Estate Tax Compliance Architecture

Real estate developers in Bangladesh operate under strict statutory withholding and VAT mandates. This module automates the entire NBR pipeline:

1. Output VAT on Customer Billing (VAT Act 2012)
Residential ≤ 1,600 sqft at 2.0%, > 1,600 sqft at 4.5%, and Commercial units at 7.5%. Official bilingual Form Mushak-6.3 generated on every milestone receipt.
2. Inter-Site Movement Mushak 6.5
Tracks transfer of rebar, cement, sand, stone chips, and subcontractor fabrication equipment between central stores and projects without double VAT taxation.
3. Income Tax Withholding (TDS under Income Tax Act 2023)
Section 89 contractor slabs (3% / 5% / 7%), Section 86 consultants (10% / 12%), Section 89 suppliers (2% to 5%) with automated Form 54 certificate generation.
4. Section 53FF Advance Income Tax (AIT)
Pre-registration AIT computation based on Rajuk/CDA/Pourashava zones per sqm/sqft before deed execution at Sub-Registry offices.
