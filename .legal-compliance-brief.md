# Legal & Compliance Management implementation brief

## Objective and audience
Extend the existing Real Estate ERP Legal Compliance Vault into a production-oriented, configurable legal operations module for legal officers, project teams, management, and auditors working in Bangladesh. Retain the existing application framework, route conventions, data access patterns, and global theme.

## Scope and content
Implement the 13 requested areas as integrated sections with unified module navigation:
1. Legal & Compliance Dashboard and drill-down indicators/filters.
2. Land Title & Ownership Verification.
3. Deed, Khatian, Mutation & Land-Tax Records.
4. Developer Registration & Renewal.
5. Land-Use Clearance & Building-Plan Approvals.
6. Approval Conditions & Expiry Tracking.
7. NOCs & Authority Correspondence.
8. Customer Sale Agreements & Transfer Documents.
9. Litigation, Disputes & Legal Notices.
10. Document Versioning & Secure Archives.
11. Compliance Calendar & Reminders.
12. Legal Review & Approval Workflows.
13. Compliance Reports & Audit Trail.

Each data section needs a server-paginated/filterable table, independent create/edit full-screen routed form (never an add form appended below a table), view/detail capability, and working backend/database CRUD. Include limit/offset and useful filters. Preserve and extend existing legal permits, conditions, cases, hearings and document behavior. Dashboard counts/drilldowns should link to source records. Model configurable checklists and approval steps; never encode a single checklist as universal legal advice. A clearance must require a documented review outcome, reviewer identity, and authorized approval, not merely uploaded files. Represent conditional approvals and explicit owners/deadlines.

## UX and design direction
Build a calm, precise enterprise legal-operations interface: compact but breathable data tables, clear status/risk hierarchy, strong page titles and breadcrumbs, distinct overview metrics, and predictable forms. Use existing shared theme tokens for all surfaces, text, borders and status treatments; maintain the same card border/radius and consistent spacing between header/sidebar/content. Use the existing Tailwind setup and installed icon package; do not add another CSS or icon dependency without need. Follow existing reusable Angular component conventions for searchable select, inputs, textareas, tabs, buttons, tables, and card containers. Every dropdown must be dynamic/searchable, backed by configurable lookups, and allow authorized quick-create at the point of use, immediately selecting the new item. Keep legal records, classifications and authorities configurable from settings. No hard-coded dropdown options.

## Typography, color, assets
Use the application's current typography and global light/dark theme palette. Do not introduce page-local color palettes or external images; this business application needs no image assets. Use theme-aware semantic status classes/tokens.

## Technical requirements
Backend is FastAPI + async SQLAlchemy + MySQL; frontend is Angular standalone components + Tailwind. Use existing module conventions, validation, dependency injection, permissions where available, and migration/schema approach. Keep APIs typed, validate inputs, expose stable paginated responses, and add tests for the new workflows and clearance safeguard. Keep Bangladesh currency/date/localization conventions where applicable. This is a configurable compliance tracker, not legal advice.

## Output path and constraints
Implement in the existing `backend/app/modules/legal/` and `frontend/src/app/features/legal/` modules, integrating the existing router and navigation rather than creating a parallel app. Preserve `/api/v1/legal` and existing application route conventions. The module's routed screens must be full-screen within the ERP shell; do not open a browser automation agent. Follow the repository's build/test commands and report validation.
